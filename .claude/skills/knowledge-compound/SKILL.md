# knowledge-compound
## Input
Task scope, applicable rules, current revision and available evidence.
## Procedure
Extract consequential decisions and lessons from completed work, label evidence and scope, update affected indexes and status, propose reusable lessons without leaking confidential data.
## Output
Structured findings or deliverables, evidence references, actual checks, limitations and next steps.
## Stop conditions
Missing authorization, conflicting requirements or unavailable required evidence: report blocker; do not invent results.
